# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in the deckent community a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone — regardless of background, identity, or level of experience. We want deckent to be a place where people can learn, contribute, and collaborate with confidence.

## Our Standards

We expect everyone in the community to:

- Use welcoming and inclusive language
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback
- Focus on what is best for the community
- Show empathy and kindness toward other community members

Conduct that harasses, demeans, or excludes others — or that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting — is not tolerated. The complete definition of expected and unacceptable behavior is the one set out by the Contributor Covenant, which this document adopts in full (see below).

## The Standard We Adopt

This Code of Conduct adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)** in its entirety. Where this summary is brief, the Contributor Covenant text governs — including its full description of community standards and its enforcement guidelines.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it privately to the maintainers at **conduct@deckent.ai**. All reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. We respect the privacy and safety of anyone who reports an incident, and we will not retaliate against good-faith reporters.

## Enforcement

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and will take fair, appropriate corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate. Following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement ladder, the response scales with severity — from a private correction, to a warning, to a temporary ban, to a permanent ban for serious or repeated violations.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any channel where someone participates in the project — and also applies when an individual is officially representing deckent in public spaces.
