## Summary
orchestra#22 is the sprint-execution periphery (task-mode dispatch, retry, restore, routing, timeout estimation, temp agent/skill generation) and shows the same **wired-core / dark-periphery** pattern as the rest of orchestra. The live path — `routeTask` (single caller `sprint-spawner.ts:1087`), `brainEstimateTimeout`/`emitTimeoutEvents` (now wired after the Sprint 280 fix), `generateProjectConventionsSkill`/`generateTempAgents`, and `createPreArchiveSnapshot`/`classifyTaskFiles` — is real, but three substantial surfaces are fully built + tested yet have zero production callers: the entire `task-retry.ts` retry module (superseded by the cascade path), the `restoreFromSnapshot` rollback half, and `generateDataDrivenSkills`. The most consequential softness is structural-guarantee erosion: an ADR-008 orchestra→cli inversion in task-mode-runner, three blocking `spawnSync` tar calls against ADR-087 in the finalize path, and a "rollback safety" snapshot that nothing can restore — each a guarantee made soft by advisory enforcement. Secondary debt: a duplicate-divergent `detectTaskType`, an unreachable LoC regex branch, and a never-populated `depHint` knob.
