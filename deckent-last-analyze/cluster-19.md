## Summary
This cluster is the Brain's sprint-lifecycle spine, and the audit shows a recurring deckent pattern: features and "self-learning"/observability surfaces are implemented + unit-tested but never wired into the live run-path. Two typed, validated, documented capabilities are entirely dead — the mid-sprint cost guard (createCostGuardMonitor) and two opt-in gates (partial-promotion, scope-auto-expand) whose flags are not even carried through config merge — alongside the whole sprint-metrics "Brain Self-Learning" block and four sprint-reporter retro "wires" that have zero consumers (second-order dormancy: code written to revive dormant code, itself dormant). The most consequential root-cause is the hardcoded-0 `boundaryViolations` metric that makes the retrospective affirmatively print "No boundary violations detected" on every sprint regardless of reality — a soft, dishonest safety signal that aligns with ADR-037's advisory-soft scope enforcement. Smaller integrity gaps (evaluate-lock fail-open, noGoRate unit confusion in dormant code, v1/v2 default conflict vs ADR-028, ADR-087 spawnSync in the plan path) reinforce that several guarantees here are documented but not enforced.
