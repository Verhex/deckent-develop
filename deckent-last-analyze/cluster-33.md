### limit-ledger.ts

## Summary
core#11 is a cluster of mostly-correct, well-documented utilities whose chief disease is **dead/unwired surface**: two entire modules (`interaction-policy.ts`, `lazy-loader.ts`) have no production consumer, the `manifest-migrator` migrate quartet is re-exported-but-uncalled (a long-known UNW-A133), and `identity-generator`'s deprecated `regenerateProjectIdentity` path is reachable only via a flag no live caller sets. The most actionable real-runtime risks are in `limit-ledger.ts` — unresolved-model cost silently collapses to $0 and null-timestamp records dodge the date filter, both undermining the "ground-truth accounting" guarantee the module advertises. A recurring deckent self-pattern shows up cleanly here: stale magic numbers (MCP 22 vs floor 31 vs reality 35), duplicate-divergent validators (core `validateIdentityAutogenScope` vs script `validateAutogenScope`), and "FromDb"-named code that actually parses a `.md` snapshot — i.e. the SSOT-vs-snapshot and advisory-vs-enforced tensions that the audit categories were designed to surface.
