## Summary
This cluster is deckent's evaluation + dispatch core, and the dominant pattern is **tested-but-unwired guarantees**: three significant correctness gates — Proof-of-Function (`verifyProofOfFunction`/`applyProofOfFunctionGate`), the Brain Layer-2 verify-delta downgrade (`applyTechDebtDowngrade`), and the TOPP B continuous dispatch planner (`planDispatch`) — are fully implemented, unit-tested, and documented as live, yet have zero production callers; their docstrings actively assert wiring that the call graph contradicts. The effect is that several advertised quality guarantees (Tier-1 real-binary verification, Brain re-check of dishonest DONE, ADR-064 wave-barrier removal) are soft in practice while reading as enforced. Secondary signals — a re-export that exists only to satisfy a goCriteria grep, a never-enforced `validateTokenUsage` whose "Sprint 140 hard NO_GO" never shipped, duplicate `getSharedMemory`/`waitForResults` definitions, and stale 216-004 duck-typing — reinforce that the subsystem accumulated capability without retiring or wiring prior layers. Resource telemetry is the clean counter-example: fully wired end-to-end.
