## Summary
This cluster is the autonomous-v2 mission-store core, and most of its public surface is genuinely wired (`runV2Engine` ← `src/cli/commands/autonomous.ts:522`, `createListMission` ← `autonomous-mission.ts:92`, `migrateBacklogJson`/`buildMissionDispatch`/`runMissionScheduler` ← `mission-engine-wire.ts`). The recurring failure mode is guarantee-softening through partial wiring: the engine gate (`autonomous.engine`) and the per-tenant fairness cap are read via off-type casts with no typed config field or default, so the entire subsystem and its enterprise fairness are reachable only by hand-injecting untyped JSON; crash recovery (`recover()`) and settlement delivery (`notify`) and the `runProcess`/`capability` brokers all exist in the contract but are never invoked by the one production composition root, so durability, delivery, and two of four work-item kinds are inert in the live runtime. Two persisted/declared concepts — `WorkItem.dependsOn` and the `MissionEventLog` event trace — are stored, typed, and tested yet never consumed, and the `parked` status (inherited from v1 without its machinery) is a terminal trap that can deadlock a migrated mission. No test file is in this cluster's read-scope, so the dead-test category is legitimately empty.
