## Summary
This cluster is the sprint dispatch spine, and the dominant signal is **accumulated dead public surface in `task-builder.ts`**: seven exported validators/helpers (`validateDirective`, `validateGroundTruthClaims`, `validateScopeFilesWrite`, `applyPersonaDomainCheck`, `inferFixMode`, `queryRelevantADRs`, `resolveTaskDependencies`) are fully implemented, doc-commented with intended wire-points, yet have zero production callers — several of them describe the exact wiring that was never done (e.g. `applyPersonaDomainCheck`'s "Wire point for sprint-planner.ts"). `sprint-utils.ts` adds three more (`resolveMaxWorkersNumeric`, `resolveDefaultUsageCli`, `detectOrphanWorkers`) plus a triple-divergent max-worker resolver, and `sprint-runner-entry.ts` ships three IPC readers exercised only by a tautological structural test. The most material correctness risk is enforcement softness: the RBAC spawn gate (`sprint-runtime.ts`) is flag-gated off through an untyped `enforce_rbac` cast and falls through to `allowed:true`, and model↔provider mismatches are logged rather than rejected — both make architectural guarantees advisory in the default configuration. The spawner itself is well-wired and defensively coded; its soft spots are documented fail-safe `catch`/silent-fallback paths that prefer resilience over surfacing misconfiguration.
